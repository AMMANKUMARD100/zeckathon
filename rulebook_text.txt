Distress Model: Trained on UCI Polish/Taiwanese Bankruptcy dataset using time-aware split.

historical pre-event years.
Manipulation Screen: Beneish M-Score, Piotroski F-Score, Altman Z-Score + soft signals (auditor resignations, promoter
pledge ratios, rating changes).
Explainability & Policy: SHAP value explanations per flagged firm; threshold tuning where a missed default costs 20x a
false positive.

4. PROBLEM STATEMENT SPECIFICATIONS (PS3 & PS4)
PS3: Financial Forecasting — Quarter-End AMC Redemption Forecast
TRACK 3
The Ask: Forecast monthly net flows for India's liquid and overnight fund categories with prediction intervals, and translate
forecasts into an optimal cash-buffer policy to minimize idle cash drag vs. redemption penalties.
Core Methodology & Rules:
Forecast Engine: 3-month forward point forecasts + 80% and 95% confidence intervals comparing classical models
(SARIMAX/ETS) against ML/DL architectures.
Exogenous Drivers: Advance tax dates (15th Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec), banking liquidity surplus/deficit, money market yields,
and market returns.
Optimization Layer: Buffer rule minimizing: Cost = Idle Cash Drag + (3 × Shortfall Penalty) backtested
over 24 months (rolling-origin).
Fund Manager Dashboard: 1-page operational interface for first-of-the-month positioning.
